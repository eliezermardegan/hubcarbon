Prepare for a more productive conversation.

Practical checklists for inventory data, project readiness and carbon-credit assessment.

Practical preparation guidance. Apply the relevant methodology and obtain specialist review for your organisation or project.

Prepare your inventory data

  1. Define the reporting period, sites and organisational boundary to discuss.
  2. Gather fuel, electricity and other activity records with units and dates.
  3. Identify relevant value-chain activities and available supplier information.
  4. Record sources, assumptions and missing information for technical review.

Prepare your climate project brief

  1. Describe the activity, location, stage and intended climate outcome.
  2. Identify ownership, rights and the parties responsible for delivery.
  3. List available feasibility studies, monitoring plans and supporting evidence.
  4. Set out the next milestone, key dependencies and support requested.

Questions for carbon-credit assessment

  1. Ask which programme, methodology and project documentation support the credits.
  2. Review additionality, quantification, permanence and reversal risks.
  3. Check registry records, vintage, issuance, retirement and delivery status.
  4. Examine safeguards, independent verification and the evidence behind intended claims.
