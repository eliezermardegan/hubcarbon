ABOUT HUB CARBON

A connected platform for the low-carbon economy.

Hub Carbon connects carbon intelligence, decarbonisation, climate projects, markets and climate finance — helping the actors around the transition find the information, capabilities and connections they need to move forward.

Talk to Hub Carbon ↗

Built to connect the parts that need to move together.

The low-carbon economy is not one market, one technology or one transaction. It is a network of companies, projects, capital, technologies, evidence and market mechanisms that increasingly need to work together.

Hub Carbon is being built around that reality: connect intelligence to action, projects to demand, and climate opportunities to the partners and funding pathways that can help them progress.

01

Connected by design

Capabilities are structured to work together rather than operate as isolated services.

02

Evidence-led

Data, measurement, traceability and disciplined processes are central to the model.

03

Market-aware

Projects and transition decisions are considered alongside demand, policy, markets and finance.

04

Built to evolve

The Hub can deepen into richer intelligence, project, marketplace and transaction layers as capabilities and evidence develop.
