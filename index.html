Before we start

Do I need a complete emissions inventory?

No. Explain the information you already have and the gaps you need to address. That helps define an appropriate starting point.

Does the service guarantee certification or funding?

No. Independent verification, credit issuance and funding decisions have their own requirements and decision-makers. Any proposed support will have a defined scope.

What should I send first?

A short description of your objective, location, project stage and timeframe. Use the initial enquiry to agree a suitable way to share confidential documents.
